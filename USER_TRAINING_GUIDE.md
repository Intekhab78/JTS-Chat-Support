# JTS Support User Training Guide

## What This System Does
JTS Support helps your team handle:

- Visitor chats
- Support tickets
- CRM leads
- Sales follow-up

This guide is for normal users like agents, sales staff, managers, and clients.

## Login
1. Open the dashboard
2. Enter your email and password
3. Login with your assigned role

Your role controls what you can see and update.

## Main Areas

### Chats
Used for live visitor conversations.

### Tickets
Used for support issues that need tracking.

### CRM
Used for leads, customer follow-up, and sales pipeline.

### Agents / Departments / Categories
Used mostly by client or manager for setup and assignment.

## For Support Agents

### Reply To Visitors
1. Open `Active Queue`
2. Select a visitor chat
3. Type your reply
4. Send message

### Convert Chat To Ticket
Use this when:
- issue needs follow-up
- customer needs status tracking
- support work is not finished in one chat

Steps:
1. Open the chat
2. Click `Convert to Ticket`
3. Fill the subject and category
4. Submit

After ticket creation:
- ticket is created automatically
- tracking link is sent to the visitor in chat

### Close Chat
After the issue is finished:
1. Open the chat
2. Click `Close Session`

## For Sales Users

### Open CRM Pipeline
1. Login as sales
2. Open `Pipeline`

You will see leads in stages like:
- New
- Qualified
- Hold
- Proposition
- Won
- Lost

### Work On Your Leads
1. Click a lead card
2. Review tickets, chats, and notes
3. Update pipeline stage
4. Add next follow-up date

### Send Email
1. Open the customer
2. Go to `Intel Notes`
3. Fill subject
4. Fill message body
5. Optional: attach a file
6. Click `Send Email`

Attachments allowed:
- JPG
- PNG
- GIF
- WEBP
- PDF

Maximum size:
- 10MB

## For Managers

### Manage CRM Leads
Managers can:
- view all CRM leads
- move leads between stages
- assign CRM owner
- update relationship status
- archive incorrect leads

### Review Tickets
Managers can:
- monitor ticket queue
- reassign ticket owners
- review overdue work

### Review Chats
Managers can:
- monitor active chats
- view conversation flow
- supervise team activity

## For Clients

### Create Staff Accounts
Clients can create:
- agents
- sales users
- managers

While creating staff, client can assign:
- websites
- department
- categories

### Setup Departments
Examples:
- Technical
- Billing
- Sales
- General

### Setup Categories
Examples:
- Technical Issues
- Billing & Payments
- Demo Request

These categories help auto-route tickets to the right department.

## CRM Lead Stages

### New
Fresh lead, not qualified yet.

### Qualified
Lead is valid and worth follow-up.

### Hold
Lead is paused for now.

### Proposition
Proposal/discussion stage.

### Won
Lead became customer/successful outcome.

### Lost
Lead is no longer active.

## Relationship Status
This is different from pipeline stage.

Possible values:
- Prospect
- Lead
- Customer
- Inactive

Use:
- pipeline stage for sales progress
- relationship status for overall customer state

## If A Lead Was Created By Mistake
Use archive instead of permanent delete in most cases.

Archive means:
- relationship status becomes inactive
- pipeline stage becomes lost

Permanent delete should be used only by high-level roles when absolutely necessary.

## Ticket Routing
Tickets can route automatically based on:
- category
- department
- assigned staff mapping

Example:
- technical ticket -> technical team
- billing ticket -> billing team
- sales inquiry -> sales team

## Common Daily Workflow

### Agent
1. Take live chat
2. Reply to visitor
3. Convert to ticket if needed
4. Close chat

### Sales
1. Open pipeline
2. Review assigned leads
3. Update stage
4. Send follow-up email
5. Schedule next follow-up

### Manager
1. Review pipeline
2. Review tickets
3. Assign owners
4. Archive mistakes

### Client
1. Review operations
2. Manage staff
3. Manage websites
4. Manage categories and departments

## If Something Is Not Working

### Chat Not Loading
- refresh page
- check backend is running

### Email Not Sending
- check customer has real email
- check SMTP settings

### CRM Not Updating
- check your role permissions
- refresh and try again

### Page Looks Old
- hard refresh with `Ctrl + F5`

## Best Practices
- Convert unresolved chat into ticket
- Keep support work and sales work separate
- Always assign CRM owner for sales leads
- Add follow-up date for active leads
- Use archive instead of delete when possible

## Quick Reminder
- Agent handles support
- Sales handles lead relationship
- Manager supervises flow
- Client manages configuration
